## Watermelon UI

(https://ui.watermelon.sh, source: https://github.com/WatermelonCorp/watermelon-platform),

`src/components/watermelon/`, and each one carries a short notice pointing here.

Quick Feedback, Inline Toast, Shimmer Button.
